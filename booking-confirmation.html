Before your appointment

Small items like receipts, loose change, or a few miscellaneous items are completely fine. However, if an area contains a significant amount of personal belongings, we'll leave that area untouched rather than moving through or handling your personal items. This helps us respect your privacy and keep your belongings exactly where you left them.

    Please also take a moment to remove anything you want to keep. Any loose items left behind that appear to be trash or unwanted may be discarded during the cleaning process. If you'd like us to leave something in the vehicle, simply set it aside or let us know.

    For the best results, clear out any areas you'd like us to clean beforehand.

What to expect at drop-off

Before we get started, we'll complete a thorough 20-point inspection of your vehicle, which takes about 5–10 minutes. This allows us to assess its current condition and document any pre-existing damage before work begins. It helps protect both you and us while setting clear expectations for what will be covered for this specific job.

After pick-up

After your detail, we'll do our best to make sure everything is cleaned to the highest standard possible. Some pre-existing damage, permanent staining, discoloration, or other wear may not be fully removable, even with professional cleaning. We're happy to provide photos of any pre-existing damage documented during the inspection if you'd like to review it. If you notice a spot we missed, please let us know. Whether you catch it at pickup or notice it in the days following your detail, don't hesitate to reach out. We won't take it personally. We'd much rather have you point it out so we can take a look and make it right. Your satisfaction is our #1 priority
